# AUDITSEO Earned Distribution Playbook v1

**Status:** operational draft
**Created:** 2026-09-08
**Scope:** public AUDITSEO research assets, beginning with the 2026 pricing and GEO/Search AI market-communication benchmarks.

## Objective

Turn proprietary research into third-party discovery signals that can be measured across:

- earned links;
- linked and unlinked brand mentions;
- referral sessions;
- Search / Search AI citations;
- branded discovery;
- qualified leads.

The objective is **not** to ask the web for backlinks. The objective is to publish evidence useful enough that journalists, specialists, companies and communities have a legitimate reason to reference AUDITSEO.

## Rules of integrity

1. **Dataset before narrative.** A snapshot cannot be rewritten after outreach begins.
2. **No fake market-wide claims.** Sample findings must remain labeled as findings from the dated sample.
3. **No pay-for-link distribution.** Do not buy links, insert links into unrelated pages or exchange reciprocal links as a condition of coverage.
4. **No negative ranking of named providers.** The research classifies observable public claims and formats; it does not score service quality.
5. **Factual corrections are welcome.** Providers can request correction of objectively inaccurate extraction/classification, with the correction logged.
6. **Corrections do not erase history.** Material post-snapshot changes create a new snapshot/version rather than silently rewriting the old one.
7. **No backlink request in correction outreach.** The reason for contacting a provider is factual integrity, not link acquisition.
8. **Evidence must stay accessible.** Article, methodology and public CSV should remain reachable from the canonical Research Hub.

## Distribution sequence

### Wave 0 — publication QA

Before external outreach:

- confirm article returns 200 and canonical is correct;
- confirm public CSV returns 200;
- confirm Article + Dataset structured data is present;
- confirm Research Hub links to the study;
- confirm the study links to methodology/data;
- create one short citation-ready summary;
- prepare 2–3 visual charts from the dataset without changing the underlying numbers;
- establish UTM campaign names before sharing links.

### Wave 1 — factual notification to included providers

Purpose: improve integrity and create legitimate awareness of the study.

Send each included provider a concise note containing:

- study URL;
- their page/offer included in the snapshot;
- snapshot date;
- the exact fields extracted/classified;
- a direct invitation to report factual extraction errors;
- clarification that this is not a request for endorsement or backlink.

Do **not** ask the provider to link to the study.

If a provider voluntarily references the research, record it as earned coverage.

### Wave 2 — founder-led publication

Publish the research from Sidney Santos' own professional identity with three different angles instead of repeating the same launch message.

Suggested sequence:

1. **Finding-led:** the most decision-useful result and why simple averages / promises mislead buyers.
2. **Method-led:** why AUDITSEO froze the dataset before writing the article.
3. **Buyer-led:** a practical checklist for comparing proposals using the benchmark.

Each post should link to the canonical study, not to a copied full-text version.

### Wave 3 — earned media / newsletters

Primary target categories:

- Brazilian marketing and advertising media;
- technology / AI publications;
- SEO / performance marketing newsletters;
- B2B marketing and SaaS publications;
- business / entrepreneurship media when the pricing angle is relevant;
- journalists writing about AI-driven search, digital acquisition or marketing services.

Pitch the **finding**, not AUDITSEO as a company announcement.

Good pitch:

> We froze 23 public SEO/GEO offers before writing the analysis and found that 'GEO pricing' currently mixes sessions, audits, sprints, retainers and software. The public CSV lets readers check every included row.

Weak pitch:

> AUDITSEO launched a new article and would like a backlink.

### Wave 4 — specialist communities

Distribute where practitioners can interrogate the methodology:

- SEO communities;
- analytics / marketing measurement groups;
- AI Search / GEO communities;
- founder / B2B growth groups where the buyer angle is relevant.

The opening should invite critique of methodology or interpretation, not promote services.

### Wave 5 — follow-up research

After coverage and corrections:

- log corrections;
- preserve snapshot v1;
- publish a changelog when justified;
- schedule a new dated snapshot rather than overwriting the existing one;
- compare market changes over time.

Longitudinal change is more cite-worthy than a single static article.

## Pitch angles by asset

### Benchmark #001 — pricing

Angle A — buyer decision:

> “Quanto custa GEO?” is a badly formed question unless the buyer first separates session, audit, sprint, monthly execution and software.

Angle B — market maturity:

> The same labels currently cover different commercial units, making price comparison unreliable without scope normalization.

Angle C — procurement:

> A R$2k audit and a R$15k/month managed program should not be compared as if they are substitutes.

### Benchmark #002 — claims / measurement

Angle A — buyer protection:

> A named framework, a timeline, a guarantee and a measurement protocol are separate properties of an offer.

Angle B — transparency:

> The public sample shows substantial variation in how GEO providers communicate uncertainty and how they propose to prove performance.

Angle C — category development:

> GEO/Search AI is becoming a commercial category before its measurement language is fully standardized.

Preferred attribution for Benchmark #001:

Preferred attribution for Benchmark #002:

Whenever possible, the citation should link to the canonical article. The public CSV can be linked when the context is specifically about the underlying data.

## UTM convention

External links controlled by AUDITSEO should use:

- `utm_source`: outlet / channel / profile identifier;
- `utm_medium=earned` for direct media/community outreach;
- `utm_medium=social` for founder social distribution;
- `utm_campaign=research_geo_2026`;
- `utm_content=pricing_benchmark` or `market_claims_benchmark`.

Do not append UTMs to links that a journalist or third party chooses to create independently.

## Measurement

Track separately:

### Distribution

- pitches sent;
- factual-notification messages sent;
- responses;
- corrections requested;
- coverage won.

### Authority

- new referring domains;
- linked mentions;
- unlinked AUDITSEO mentions;
- citations of the dataset/study;
- branded searches;
- citations / mentions in monitored Search AI prompts.

### Acquisition

- referral sessions;
- Search / Search AI sessions;
- diagnostic starts;
- qualified leads;
- meetings;
- revenue attribution when available.

Do not collapse these into one vanity score.

## What success looks like

The strongest outcome is not “X backlinks”.

The target narrative is:

> A qualified prospect searched or asked an AI about SEO + Search AI/GEO, found an AUDITSEO study or a third-party reference to that study, validated the methodology and arrived at a meeting already treating AUDITSEO as a credible specialist.

That outcome should be recorded as evidence in Case Study #001 when it actually happens.
