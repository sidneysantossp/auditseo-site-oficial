# AUDITSEO — Problem-Led Authority Sprint

**Intervention date:** 2026-10-02  
**Case:** Case Study #001 — Authority → Lead  
**Trigger:** Search AI Cycle 001 partial checkpoint  
**Public-metric policy:** the intervention is public; incomplete Cycle 001 percentages remain internal until the protocol release gate is complete.

## Why this sprint exists

The first problem-led Search AI observations showed a clear acquisition gap: AUDITSEO could enter some provider-discovery prompts, but the problem-led module did not produce AUDITSEO mentions in the completed three-interface subset.

The correct intervention is not to create many new URLs. The repository already contains strong documents for the four priority problems. The sprint therefore preserves those canonicals and strengthens intent match, direct answers, methodology and internal relationships.

## What was deliberately not done

- no new near-duplicate landing pages;
- no changes to canonical URLs;
- no doorway pages;
- no publication of incomplete Observatory percentages inside the articles;
- no claim that schema, links, mentions or any other single intervention guarantees AI citation;
- no replacement of official platform documentation with proprietary causal claims.

## Internal-link architecture

The four canonical documents now form a problem-led cluster:

1. competitor gap;
2. Google-to-AI gap;
3. measurement;
4. entity structure.

The cluster also connects outward to the public benchmark protocol and Case Study #001 where context is useful.

## Measurement plan

Do not judge the intervention from immediate Search AI responses.

Re-test only after a plausible recrawl/indexing window, using the exact frozen prompt wording and the same classification rules.

Track separately:

- Mention Rate;
- Owned Citation Rate;
- Provider Consideration;
- Recommendation Rate;
- Source Share;
- Entity Accuracy;
- branded Search Console demand;
- referral and qualified-lead evidence when observable.

## Success condition

The sprint succeeds if, in a later comparable cycle, problem-led prompts begin to recover AUDITSEO or its documents more consistently **without degrading factual accuracy or introducing unsupported claims**.

A positive result is evidence of change inside the declared sample. It is not, by itself, proof that this sprint caused the change.
