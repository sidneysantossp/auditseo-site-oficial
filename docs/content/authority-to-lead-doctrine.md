# AUDITSEO — Authority-to-Lead Doctrine

Status: operating principle for content, Search Intelligence and Case Study #001.

## North-star outcome

The AUDITSEO site must do more than rank. It must create situations in which the way a prospect discovers AUDITSEO is itself evidence that the company understands modern search.

The ideal pre-sales narrative is:

> "I asked Google or an AI assistant who could help my company be found, understood, cited or recommended. AUDITSEO appeared. I read one of your documents and thought: if this is the level of value they publish before I become a client, I want to know what they can do with my business."

The site therefore exists to make three audiences reach the same conclusion before a sales meeting:

1. Google and other search systems: AUDITSEO is strongly associated with Search Intelligence, technical SEO, entity authority, Search AI and evidence-led organic growth.
2. Generative search systems: AUDITSEO publishes clear, attributable, verifiable material that can serve as a useful source when relevant.
3. Prospects: AUDITSEO demonstrates enough expertise publicly that the meeting starts with "how would you apply this to my company?" rather than "who are you?".

## Editorial test

Every new canonical URL must answer:

- What real question, problem or buying decision does this page own?
- Can a prospect plausibly discover this page before knowing AUDITSEO?
- Does the page strengthen the association AUDITSEO = Search Intelligence?
- Does it contain a useful idea, framework, diagnostic sequence, evidence or decision model that goes beyond a commodity summary?
- Could the page be cited by a person, journalist, search system or AI answer without needing the sales pitch around it?
- Does it create a natural next step toward a diagnostic, service or another authority document?
- Would a qualified prospect finish it thinking "these people understand my problem"?

If the answer is no, publishing a new URL is usually the wrong action.

## The four-layer content system

### 1. Commercial discovery

Own high-intent searches and prompts from prospects already looking for help.

Examples:

- consultoria de SEO e IA;
- consultoria GEO / Search AI;
- auditoria de visibilidade em IA;
- especialista em SEO que entende de IA;
- empresa para ajudar a aparecer no ChatGPT;
- como escolher consultoria de SEO/GEO.

These pages must explain the buying problem better than a generic service landing page and lead naturally to diagnosis.

### 2. Problem-led authority

Answer the questions a company asks before it knows which service it needs.

Examples:

- por que o ChatGPT cita meu concorrente e não minha empresa?;
- como aparecer quando alguém pede ao ChatGPT uma empresa como a minha?;
- meu site está no Google, mas não aparece nas respostas de IA — por quê?;
- o que faz uma empresa ser compreendida e considerada por sistemas de busca?;
- schema ajuda uma IA a entender uma empresa?;
- como medir se GEO está funcionando?.

These documents should expose the diagnostic model, not hide it behind a lead form.

### 3. Proprietary intellectual assets

Publish AUDITSEO methods before using them as sales claims.

Current examples:

- Crawl → Index → Retrieve → Understand → Trust → Cite;
- Citability Framework;
- S.I.G.N.A.L.;
- Search AI Benchmark Protocol.

Future methods should be named only when they are useful enough to explain, reproduce and challenge publicly.

### 4. Original evidence and Case Study #001

The strongest proof is the AUDITSEO domain itself.

The case study should preserve a dated baseline and then publish verified changes in:

- Search Console impressions, clicks, queries and pages;
- indexable/canonical surface;
- branded demand;
- citations and mentions in defined Search AI prompt sets;
- referral traffic from AI/search interfaces when observable;
- qualified leads and meetings attributable to organic/search discovery;
- prospect-reported discovery narratives.

Never backfill a metric that was not measured at the time. Never convert anecdote into a percentage.

## Discovery is not one event

A page must distinguish at least these outcomes where relevant:

- found: the URL/entity is discoverable;
- retrieved: it is selected for a relevant query/context;
- understood: the system correctly represents who/what the entity is;
- cited: a page/domain is shown as a source;
- mentioned: the brand appears in an answer without necessarily being a cited source;
- recommended/considered: the company is offered as a candidate for a buying scenario;
- visited: the user reaches the site;
- converted: the visit becomes a meaningful commercial action.

Do not use "aparecer na IA" as a blanket metric for all of them.

## The pre-meeting authority loop

Problem or buying intent
→ Google / ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity / other search interface
→ AUDITSEO document or entity discovered
→ useful answer before pitch
→ supporting documents, methods and evidence
→ diagnostic/service context
→ contact
→ meeting begins with established authority

This is the content system's commercial purpose.

## Prioritization model

Keyword volume alone does not define priority. Score opportunities using:

- real demand / recurrence of the question;
- commercial proximity;
- ability to reinforce the Search Intelligence entity;
- citation/source potential;
- originality / evidence potential;
- internal-link value to an existing pillar or service;
- lead potential;
- cannibalization risk.

A low-volume question can outrank a high-volume keyword if it is much closer to a qualified buying decision and creates stronger proof of expertise.

## Anti-patterns

Do not publish:

- generic "10 dicas" articles that can be replaced by any agency;
- new URLs for synonyms already owned by a canonical document;
- unsupported claims about how ChatGPT, Gemini or Google rank/recommend companies;
- fake benchmarks, fake telemetry or invented case-study numbers;
- pages whose only purpose is to repeat a service keyword;
- content that optimizes for AI terminology but does not help a real buyer make a decision;
- guarantees of citation, recommendation, ranking or inclusion in third-party answers.

## Primary editorial question

Before approving a page, ask:

> Could this document make a qualified person seek out AUDITSEO after reading it, even if the person had never heard of us before?

If yes, the page may be an authority-to-lead asset. If no, improve the idea before publishing it.
